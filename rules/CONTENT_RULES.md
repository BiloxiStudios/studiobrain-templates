# Content guidance

These rules check structure and consistency. They do not name a setting.

Content must fit the entities, history, technology, and tone this project has recorded. Keep relationships consistent. Use the project's names and terms. Include enough concrete detail to picture the thing, and no detail that belongs to another world.

Balance the world's facts with the people in the scene. Support both the story and, when the project has play, the play. Respect the timeline the project uses.
