# Smoky Chickpea Tacos

## Why it is on the menu

A fast main that holds well on the line and suits most allergen requests.

## Method notes

The pickled onion keeps for five days chilled. Roast the chickpeas to order; they soften if held.

## Plating

Two tacos per plate, crema under and over, a lime wedge on the side.

## Substitutions

Leave out the sesame seeds for a sesame allergy. Use wheat-free tortillas only.
